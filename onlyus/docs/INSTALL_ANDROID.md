# Installing ONLYUS on Android

ONLYUS is distributed as a private signed APK for just the two of you.

## 1. Download the APK
1. Open the GitHub Release link shared with you (or download `ONLYUS-v1.0.0.apk` directly).
2. Tap the file to start downloading.

## 2. Allow Installing Unknown Apps
1. When you tap the downloaded `.apk`, Android may ask to allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps.
2. Tap **Settings** → toggle **Allow from this source** ON → press **Back**.
3. Tap **Install**.

## 3. Google Play Protect Notice
Because ONLYUS is a private two-person app and is not listed on the public Google Play Store, Play Protect may show *"Unknown developer"* or *"Scan app"*.
- Tap **More details → Install anyway** (or **Scan app** and then **Install**).

## 4. Sign In
1. Open **ONLYUS**.
2. Sign in with your pre-approved email and password.
   *(Anyone can install the APK, but only the two pre-approved partner accounts can sign in.)*

## 5. Recommended Phone Settings (for Reliable Calls & Notifications)
1. **Notifications:** Allow notifications when prompted so messages and calls arrive on time.
2. **Battery Optimization:** Go to **Settings → Apps → ONLYUS → Battery** and select **Unrestricted** (especially on Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, OnePlus, and Samsung phones) so background notifications and incoming calls are not killed by aggressive OEM battery savers.
